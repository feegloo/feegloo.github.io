# HRV Breathing privacy policy

Public URL: https://aleksanderfigiel.pl/ios-breathing-privacy-policy

Static English HTML for the App Store Privacy Policy URL. No JavaScript,
analytics, external fonts or cookies are added by the page.

App statements were checked against `feegloo/vibe-ios-app-breathing` main commit
`c19061dc9d7f7d9785b737070535a38795a849f0`: app Swift sources,
Info.plist and project.yml. The app stores speed, sound selection and tutorial
completion in local UserDefaults. No network clients or third-party SDKs were found.

The separate [support service](support.md) collects voluntarily submitted email,
app name and message in Supabase. The policy describes this separately from
on-device app behavior, plus website infrastructure, retention, deletion requests
and privacy rights. Support retention and deletion require handling by the operator;
no scheduled deletion job is added.

Update this policy and App Store privacy disclosures if SDKs, networking,
health measurements or other data collection are added. The app must also expose
an accessible link to the policy under Apple's review requirements; this website
change does not add a link inside the app.

Preview with `python3 -m http.server 8000` from the repository root and open
http://localhost:8000/ios-breathing-privacy-policy/.
