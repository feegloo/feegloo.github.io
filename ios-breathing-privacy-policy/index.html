HRV BREATHING & MEDITATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

HRV Breathing & Meditation is developed by Aleksander Figiel, Poland. This policy explains how the app and its optional support service handle your information. For privacy questions, contact me through the support form.

Information handled by the app

The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

The app stores your breathing pace, selected sound and tutorial completion locally on your device. These settings are not sent to the developer. They may be included in device backups managed by Apple according to your device settings.

The app does not measure your heart rate or HRV, access HealthKit, record microphone audio, or access your location, contacts or photos.

Audio and playback controls

Sounds are generated on your device. Background playback, Lock Screen controls and supported headphone controls use Apple's system audio features. Playback information is provided to iOS to display the controls; it is not sent to the developer.

Optional support requests

If you use the support form, you voluntarily provide your email address, app name and message. The service also stores a request ID, submission time and handling status. This information is used to respond to your request and resolve problems. Please do not include passwords, medical records or other sensitive information.

Support requests are stored using Supabase in the project's Frankfurt, Germany database region. Requests are not publicly readable. Support information is not sold or used for advertising.

Where the GDPR applies, support information is processed on the basis of legitimate interests in responding to requests, maintaining the service and preventing abuse (Article 6(1)(f)). Providing it is optional, but without an email address and message I cannot respond to a support request.

Service providers and website access

The policy and support website are hosted by GitHub Pages. Supabase provides storage and infrastructure for support requests. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure their services. This policy page and the support page contain no analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Provider operations may involve processing outside the European Economic Area. Information about their privacy practices and international transfer safeguards is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement and Supabase Privacy Policy.

Apple may separately process App Store, TestFlight or diagnostic information according to your choices and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local app settings remain on your device until removed with the app or through device data management. Copies in device backups are governed by your Apple backup settings.

Support information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request and related follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. You can request deletion through the support form, using the email address associated with your request. Deleted information may remain in provider backups until those backups expire under the provider's retention schedule.

Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact me through the support form to exercise these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including UODO in Poland. No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made using support information.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
